How the map works in Essex

Essex isn't one market. It's dozens.

Nobody searches “electrician Essex”. They search “electrician Chelmsford”, or just “electrician near me” from their kitchen in Brentwood. Each town has its own map pack, with its own three winners.

Google ranks your profile strongest around where your business is based, then weaker the further out you go. So a Chelmsford trade can win Chelmsford and the villages around it, but won't take the Colchester map pack just by listing Colchester as a service area.

That's how we work across Essex: we focus your profile on your base town and its neighbours, where you can genuinely win, and use your website to pick up searches from the wider area. It's not as flashy as promising the whole county, but it's what gets the phone ringing.

Honest about the map

If an agency promises you'll rank across the whole of Essex from one address, be wary. The map pack doesn't work like that, and the only way to fake it is with fake addresses — which gets profiles suspended.

Straight answers

Questions trades ask us.

Can you get me ranking in more than one Essex town?

In the map pack, you'll rank strongest in and around your base town, and we'll push that as far as it'll genuinely go. For towns further away, we build pages on your website so you can show up in the normal search results there too.

I've got a yard in one town but do most jobs in another. Which do we target?

Your profile has to use your real address, so the map pack will favour the town you're based in. We'll look at where your best jobs come from and set up your profile and website to give you the strongest chance in both.

How long does it take?

Quieter towns can move within weeks; busier ones like Chelmsford take longer. You'll get a straight answer for your trade and town in the free audit, and our 90-day guarantee covers one main search we agree up front.

Do I need a website to rank on Google Maps?

Not to appear — plenty of trades rank with just a Google Business Profile. But a fast, simple website that names the towns you work in helps you climb higher and turns more searches into calls. If you need one, we can build it.

Will you work with my competitor?

No. We take on one business per trade, per town. If you're our roofer in a town, we won't take on another roofer there.
