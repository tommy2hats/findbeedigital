How the map works in Harlow

Why Harlow jobs are won on the map.

When someone in Harlow searches “plumber” or “roofer near me”, Google shows three businesses on a map before anything else. That's where most of the calls go. Get into those three and the phone rings; sit at number six and you're scrolled past.

Those three aren't the same for everyone. Google weighs how close you are to the person searching, so someone in Church Langley can see a different top 3 to someone in Great Parndon. That's why we set your profile up around where you're actually based and the parts of Harlow you actually cover.

On top of distance, Google looks at relevance (do your categories, services and description match the search?) and prominence (how many genuine reviews you have, and how consistently your details appear across the web). Those two are where we do most of the work.

Working from home? That's fine.

Most Harlow trades don't have a shop, and Google doesn't expect you to. You can be a service-area business with your home address hidden and still rank in the map pack.

What you can't do is use a fake or virtual address to look closer to a town. Google suspends profiles for that, and we don't do it.

Straight answers

Questions trades ask us.

How long does it take to get into the top 3 in Harlow?

It depends on your trade and how strong the competition is. A Harlow trade with a well-set-up profile and a steady flow of reviews can usually see real movement within a few weeks, with the bigger gains over the first few months. That's why our guarantee is 90 days, for one main search we agree with you up front.

I'm on the edge of Harlow. Can I still rank there?

Usually, yes. Google ranks you strongest around where your business is based, so if you're in Sawbridgeworth or Roydon you can still show up for much of Harlow. We'll check where you appear now in the free audit and tell you honestly what's realistic.

My profile is old and has a few bad reviews. Can you help?

Yes. Bad reviews can't be deleted, but ones that break Google's rules can be reported, and the rest get pushed down by a steady flow of genuine new ones. We also go carefully with older profiles — big changes made all at once can trigger re-verification.

Do I need a website to rank on Google Maps?

Not to appear — plenty of trades rank with just a Google Business Profile. But a fast, simple website that names the towns you work in helps you climb higher and turns more searches into calls. If you need one, we can build it.

Will you work with my competitor?

No. We take on one business per trade, per town. If you're our roofer in a town, we won't take on another roofer there.
